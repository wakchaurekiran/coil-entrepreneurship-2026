The 5-stage COIL learning cycle

Each week moves teams one stage forward, and each live session builds on the one before it.

Connect→Compare→Collaborate→Create→Communicate

Every session runs the same rhythm: a short mini-lecture, a team breakout, a plenary or peer-review round, and a briefing on the week's self-paced work.

Notes for students

Attend every session

All registered students are strongly encouraged to attend each live session. Every session builds on the previous one, and active participation is essential.

Work as a team

Each week your team completes activities based on the session topic and uploads them to the shared folder.

Open the upload folder

Come prepared

Be ready to discuss, share ideas and collaborate with peers from partner institutions. Cite a source and year for any market figure you use.
