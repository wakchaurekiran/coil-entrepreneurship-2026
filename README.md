# COIL Entrepreneurship & Startup Sessions 2026

Student information page for the COIL 5-Week Entrepreneurship & Startup Sessions (Track 3 – Idea Generation to Business Plan), led by UTAS-Shinas, Oman, with partner institutions from Oman, the Philippines and India.

**Live page:** https://wakchaurekiran.github.io/coil-entrepreneurship-2026/

## What's on the page
- Program overview and the 5-stage COIL learning cycle
- Weekly schedule (17 Nov – 15 Dec 2026, Tuesdays 12:00–2:00 PM Oman time) with India and Philippines times
- Microsoft Teams links, meeting IDs and passcodes for each session
- Weekly deliverables and assessment weights
- 22 mixed-institution student teams with search and institution filter
- Notes for students, the Google Drive upload folder and coordinator contact

## Updating
Edit `index.html` and commit; GitHub Pages republishes within a minute or two.
Team data is the `TEAMS` array near the bottom of `index.html`.
